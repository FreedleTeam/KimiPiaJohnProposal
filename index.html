Finding your place in Park Hill

How I propose we work together on a search that will take more than watching the MLS.

What we're looking for

Two homes, side by side

Adjacent homes in Park Hill, one for each family.

One home, room to build

A single property on a lot large enough for a 1,000 sq ft ADU, with yard left over for the boys to play.

It's both or nothing. We won't buy one home without a clear path to the other.

Search area

17th Avenue to 27th Avenue, from Ash Street east to Kearney Street.

How we'll work together

Two agreements

One buyer agency agreement for Pia & John, and one for Kimi & Peter.

A two-year term

I hope this takes less than a year. The right homes may take time to come up, so I'm asking for two. If we all want to keep going after that, we'll talk then.

2.8% on each property

Compensation is 2.8% of the purchase price on each home purchased. It's typically paid by the seller. In the rare case a seller isn't offering compensation, the buyer covers it.

If I'm not living up to the commitments in this proposal, you can end the agreement early.

The right two homes may never be on the market at the same time.

So we'll look on the MLS and off it.

On the market

I watch the MLS

I track new listings in the search area and send you anything that could fit, with my honest take on whether it's worth a look.

Touring is your call

Once you confirm a home is worth your time, I'll tour it. Come with me, or let me see it first and save you the trip.

Off the market

Many of the homes that could work for you won't be listed when we need them. This is how I find them anyway.

Neighbor outreach

For any home you like, I knock on the neighbors' doors and leave handwritten notes.

Targeted mail

Letters and postcards to the 250 homes in the area that data shows are most likely to sell.

Open houses

One per month in Park Hill, February through September, with door knocking nearby to meet neighbors and find homes before they list.

Agent outreach

Regular contact with agents who list in Park Hill, to hear about homes before they hit the market.

Facebook

A monthly post in the Park Hill community Facebook group.

Instagram and YouTube

Videos made for Park Hill residents, so future sellers know who to call.

Everything we send talks about one home

Every letter, postcard, note and post describes a buyer looking for one home in Park Hill. The full plan stays between us.

It keeps things simple for sellers

A request for two side-by-side homes can sound complicated or unlikely. One buyer, one home is easy to respond to.

It protects your negotiating position

If a seller knows their house completes a pair, they know we need it. That costs you leverage.
